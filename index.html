How we choose what to compare

These guides are written around family setup friction, not brand hype. Product links stay pending until affiliate approval and tracking are confirmed.

Ease of useCan an older parent answer, read, charge, and recover from confusion with less help?
Family handoffCan adult children set it up, explain it calmly, and review problems later?
Total costWe look at device price, monthly plans, activation fees, cancellation rules, and return policies.
Support limitsWe call out Wi-Fi, battery, account recovery, false alerts, missed calls, and confusing prompts.
Safety wordingDevices can reduce friction, but they cannot guarantee safety, emergency response, or scam protection.
