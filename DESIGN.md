# Parent Tech Checklist Design System

## Positioning

Parent Tech Checklist should feel like a practical consumer buying guide for US families helping aging parents choose and set up everyday technology. The visual language should borrow from trusted editorial review sites, not medical services, emergency providers, or AI product pages.

## Design Direction

- Editorial buying guide with calm consumer-tech polish.
- Clear comparison-first layouts inspired by product review and checklist sites.
- Warm family context through photography, but no sentimental hero treatment.
- Conservative claims, visible disclosure, and no vendor CTAs until affiliate approval is confirmed.

## Typography

- Use system sans-serif for speed and consistency.
- H1 is large and editorial, but never uses negative letter spacing.
- Page headings should describe the user decision directly.
- Body text should stay practical and plain.
- Avoid salesy claims, urgency pressure, and medical-sounding certainty.

## Layout

- Keep cards at 8px radius or less.
- Use full-width bands for page sections.
- Use cards only for repeated guides, checklist blocks, summaries, or compact panels.
- Prefer comparison tables and checklist grids over decorative layouts.
- On mobile, the first viewport must show the hero and hint at the next section.

## Components

- Primary CTA: checklist or guide page.
- Secondary CTA: YouTube channel or matching video.
- Trust strip: plain facts about the editorial approach.
- Decision path cards: direct links based on the family's problem.
- Comparison tables: concise and horizontally scrollable on small screens.
- Disclosure panel: visible on every guide page.
- Pending affiliate status: show pending or no vendor link until approval and tracking are confirmed.

## Voice

- Use "compare", "reduce friction", "help families decide", and "setup checklist".
- Avoid "prevent", "guarantee", "protects from every scam", "medical-grade", or emergency promises.
- Make limitations easy to see before any buying decision.

## Anti-patterns

- No dark AI gradient hero.
- No medical clinic look.
- No oversized decorative cards nested inside sections.
- No unapproved affiliate links.
- No claims that a device guarantees safety, scam prevention, or emergency response.
