What should our family do?

Before resetting the phone

  1. Check whether the service has an official incident.
  2. Try a regular phone call or text message.
  3. Confirm the phone still has Wi-Fi or cellular service.
  4. Wait for the next official update if the source is unavailable.
  5. Reset only after the incident is resolved or ruled out.

Source freshness rules

Google, Zoom, and Apple turn unavailable after 10 minutes without a successful check. FTC and CISA turn unavailable after 6 hours. CPSC turns unavailable after 12 hours. An expired snapshot is never shown as normal.
