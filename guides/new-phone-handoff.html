The family question

What must work before the old phone is erased? Move to a new phone without losing access, photos, or trusted contacts. This guide is for a household where a new phone is ready but essential calls, photos or recovery paths may still depend on the old one. For A Calm New Phone Handoff, the goal is one repeatable task the account owner understands, not a broad device overhaul.

Before you begin

Charge both phones, confirm a recent backup and keep the old device signed in until testing is complete. Before changing anything for A Calm New Phone Handoff, agree on the exact result and keep a working contact method available in case the task needs to pause.

Three safe steps

1. Back up before the handoff.

Begin with the parent beside you and back up before the handoff. For a calm new phone handoff, record which data transferred and which call, message and recovery tests passed; keep passwords, recovery codes and private answers off the worksheet. The checkpoint is simple: the parent can complete the three most important tasks on the new phone.

2. Test calls and account recovery.

Next, test calls and account recovery. For a calm new phone handoff, compare what appears on the device with the official guidance below, not with instructions from an incoming message or caller. Do not erase, trade in or remove the old phone from accounts until every critical test passes.

3. Erase the old phone only after verification.

Finish by erase the old phone only after verification. Ask the parent to repeat the result without coaching, then add the date beside which data transferred and which call, message and recovery tests passed. If the a calm new phone handoff task cannot be repeated, return to the last working state and use the official support route.

If it does not go as expected

Do not erase, trade in or remove the old phone from accounts until every critical test passes. While working on A Calm New Phone Handoff, a warning, unfamiliar login screen or unexpected request for private information is a reason to pause, document what is visible and return through an official support page.

Make the family handoff clear

Write down which data transferred and which call, message and recovery tests passed and the name of the approved helper. The A Calm New Phone Handoff handoff is complete only when the parent can complete the three most important tasks on the new phone. Review this topic note after a relevant device, account or caregiver change.
