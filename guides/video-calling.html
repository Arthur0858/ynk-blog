Setup checklist

Make one app primary.

Pick FaceTime, WhatsApp, Google Meet, or another family standard. Avoid asking the parent to remember several apps.

Test answer flow.

Run a real call from another room and confirm the parent can answer, hear, speak, and end the call.

Reduce charger friction.

Use a visible charging spot, stand, or dock so the device is ready when family calls.

Keep privacy visible.

Explain when the camera is on and how to decline a call without panic.

Family connection routine

Video calling usually works better when the family also agrees on one routine for photo sharing, missed calls, and follow-up. Keep one app primary, keep one charger visible, and decide who will check updates or account recovery if the parent gets stuck.

The device matters, but the repeatable family routine matters more. If your parent needs to search for the app, move between several chargers, or guess who to call for help, the setup still has friction.
