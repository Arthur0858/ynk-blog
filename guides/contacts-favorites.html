The family question

Can a parent reach the right person in one tap? Put a small trusted circle one tap away. This guide is for a household where important family contacts are buried in a long address book. For Put the Right People in Favorites, the goal is one repeatable task the account owner understands, not a broad device overhaul.

Before you begin

Ask the parent which people they actually call for everyday help and emergencies; do not choose on their behalf. Before changing anything for Put the Right People in Favorites, agree on the exact result and keep a working contact method available in case the task needs to pause.

Three safe steps

1. Choose three to five trusted people.

Begin with the parent beside you and choose three to five trusted people. For put the right people in favorites, record the three-to-five visible names and the screen where each can be reached; keep passwords, recovery codes and private answers off the worksheet. The checkpoint is simple: the parent can place and end one test call independently.

2. Use clear names and photos.

Next, use clear names and photos. For put the right people in favorites, compare what appears on the device with the official guidance below, not with instructions from an incoming message or caller. Stop if editing a contact would overwrite another person's number or merge two different people.

3. Test calls from the lock and home screens.

Finish by test calls from the lock and home screens. Ask the parent to repeat the result without coaching, then add the date beside the three-to-five visible names and the screen where each can be reached. If the put the right people in favorites task cannot be repeated, return to the last working state and use the official support route.

If it does not go as expected

Stop if editing a contact would overwrite another person's number or merge two different people. While working on Put the Right People in Favorites, a warning, unfamiliar login screen or unexpected request for private information is a reason to pause, document what is visible and return through an official support page.

Make the family handoff clear

Write down the three-to-five visible names and the screen where each can be reached and the name of the approved helper. The Put the Right People in Favorites handoff is complete only when the parent can place and end one test call independently. Review this topic note after a relevant device, account or caregiver change.
