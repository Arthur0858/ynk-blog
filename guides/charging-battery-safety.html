The family question

Are cables batteries and charging locations in good condition? Reduce charging risk with sound cables, open surfaces, and a simple routine. This guide is for a household where a cable, charger or battery becomes damaged, swollen or unusually hot. For A Safer Charging Station, the goal is one repeatable task the account owner understands, not a broad device overhaul.

Before you begin

Move charging to a hard open surface and inspect cables and devices while they are cool and unplugged. Before changing anything for A Safer Charging Station, agree on the exact result and keep a working contact method available in case the task needs to pause.

Three safe steps

1. Remove damaged cables.

Begin with the parent beside you and remove damaged cables. For a safer charging station, record the device model, charger used and any heat, swelling or damage observed; keep passwords, recovery codes and private answers off the worksheet. The checkpoint is simple: normal charging stays cool enough to touch and uses undamaged equipment.

2. Keep chargers off soft furniture.

Next, keep chargers off soft furniture. For a safer charging station, compare what appears on the device with the official guidance below, not with instructions from an incoming message or caller. Stop using and move away from a swelling, smoking, leaking or rapidly heating battery; follow local emergency guidance.

3. Stop using swollen or unusually hot batteries.

Finish by stop using swollen or unusually hot batteries. Ask the parent to repeat the result without coaching, then add the date beside the device model, charger used and any heat, swelling or damage observed. If the a safer charging station task cannot be repeated, return to the last working state and use the official support route.

If it does not go as expected

Stop using and move away from a swelling, smoking, leaking or rapidly heating battery; follow local emergency guidance. While working on A Safer Charging Station, a warning, unfamiliar login screen or unexpected request for private information is a reason to pause, document what is visible and return through an official support page.

Make the family handoff clear

Write down the device model, charger used and any heat, swelling or damage observed and the name of the approved helper. The A Safer Charging Station handoff is complete only when normal charging stays cool enough to touch and uses undamaged equipment. Review this topic note after a relevant device, account or caregiver change.

Printable family checklist

  • Are cables batteries and charging locations in good condition?
  • Remove damaged cables.
  • Keep chargers off soft furniture.
  • Stop using swollen or unusually hot batteries.
  • We recorded the device model, charger used and any heat, swelling or damage observed.
  • The account owner understood every change.
  • We tested one normal task after the change.
