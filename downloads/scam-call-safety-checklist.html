Aging Parent Tech Guide

Scam Call Safety Checklist

Use this to reduce call and text friction. No tool can stop every scam, so the family rule and review routine matter as much as the app or setting.

Family Pause Rule

If a call or text asks for money, passwords, codes, gift cards, urgent action, or a link tap: stop, hang up, and call family first.

Monthly Review

Review missed calls with the parent. Review blocked calls for important numbers. Remove confusing apps or prompts.
Practice the pause rule once. Confirm bank and pharmacy contact numbers. Update family emergency contacts if needed.
