Scam Call Family Pause Rule Pack

Call blockers can reduce noise, but pressure is often the real problem. Give your parent one rule they can remember before they tap, reply, or share information.

Family pause rule

If it asks for money, passwords, codes, account access, urgent action, gift cards, or a link: pause and ask family first.

Practice scripts

Suspicious textDo not tap the link.Open the official app or website separately.Send a screenshot to family if unsure.
Urgent callerHang up calmly.Call the known family number or official number separately.Do not read codes aloud.
Before filtersSave doctors, pharmacy, bank, family, and neighbors.Review missed calls after changing settings.
Monthly reviewCheck blocked calls and confusing prompts.Practice one sample message without blame.
