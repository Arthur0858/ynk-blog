Parent Tech Quick-Start Kit

Video Call Setup Checklist

Use this before choosing a phone, tablet, or smart display. The goal is an easier answer path, not more apps to manage.

Compare answer friction

Device pathCan help whenSetup limit
PhoneThe parent already carries and charges it.Small screens and app clutter can make answering harder.
TabletLarger screen helps reading faces and buttons.Needs a stable charging spot and simple home screen.
Smart displayWorks well in one room for routine family calls.Account setup, privacy expectations, and Wi-Fi matter.

Setup checklist

Choose one video call app or device path.Place the device where charging is easy.
Save family contacts and remove extra icons.Test answer, hang up, mute, and volume.
Practice at the same time of day twice.Write one sentence for what to tap when family calls.
Decide who fixes Wi-Fi, passwords, and missed calls.Review after one week and simplify prompts.
