Parent Tech Quick-Start Kit

7-Day Parent Tech Setup Plan

Use this one-week workflow before buying devices or changing settings for an aging parent. The goal is less friction, clearer expectations, and a calmer handoff.

This is general technology education, not medical, legal, financial, cybersecurity, or emergency advice. No device or checklist can guarantee safety or stop every scam.

Day-by-day plan

Day 1: Pick the main problemChoose one priority: calls, scam texts, video calls, or living-alone check-ins.Write the parent’s daily task in plain words.
Day 2: List trusted contactsAdd family, doctors, pharmacy, bank, neighbors, and care contacts.Confirm which calls must always get through.
Day 3: Compare fewer optionsCompare setup steps, monthly cost, battery, account recovery, and return policy.Remove products that create weekly support work.
Day 4: Set up the first screenIncrease text size, sound, and contrast.Put only daily apps or contacts on the first screen.
Day 5: Practice the real routinePractice answer, call back, video call, charge, and suspicious text handling.Repeat with calm language, not a lecture.
Day 6: Explain limitsWrite what the device can help with and what it cannot promise.Decide who reviews missed alerts or blocked calls.
Day 7: Review and simplifyRemove confusing prompts, unused apps, and extra notifications.Schedule a monthly 10-minute review.
Keep visiblePrint this sheet and keep it near the charger or family notes.Update it when costs, contacts, or routines change.
