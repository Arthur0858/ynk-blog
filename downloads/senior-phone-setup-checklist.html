Senior Phone Setup Checklist

Use this before buying or handing over a phone. The goal is a phone an aging parent can answer, read, charge, and recover from confusion with less help.

1. Buying Fit

Primary jobs

Voice calls are easy to answer. Text is readable indoors and outdoors. Photos or video calls are actually needed.

Cost and support

Monthly plan and fees are clear. Return policy is written down. Family knows who handles account recovery.

2. Setup Before Handoff

3. First Week Review

Parent can answer a call without coaching. Parent can charge the phone in the usual spot. Family reviewed missed calls and confusing prompts.
Volume, ringtone, and vibration are comfortable. Emergency contacts are accurate. Unneeded notifications are reduced.
