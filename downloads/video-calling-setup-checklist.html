Parent Tech Checklist

Video Calling Setup Checklist

Use this before buying a phone, tablet, or smart display for family calls. Choose the setup that makes answering easiest for the parent who will use it.

Device Fit

OptionGood fit whenSetup check
Smart displayCalls happen from a kitchen, living room, or fixed spot.[ ] Test Wi-Fi, camera angle, volume, and privacy controls.
PhoneThe parent already answers mobile calls comfortably.[ ] Pin the video app and add family favorites.
TabletA larger screen helps, and the parent can keep it charged.[ ] Use a visible charger, stand, or dock.

Setup Checklist

Choose one primary video app. Remove duplicate calling apps from the first screen. Add family contacts as favorites.
Run a real test call from another room. Confirm parent can answer, hear, speak, and end the call. Explain when the camera is on.

One Week Review

Device was charged when family called. Parent knew whether to answer or decline. Family adjusted volume and camera angle.
Notifications were not confusing. Photo sharing worked as expected. Privacy controls were explained calmly.
