About

Calm technology help for families

Parent Tech Checklist helps older adults and trusted family helpers make one understandable technology decision at a time.

Who the site is for

The primary reader is an adult child, relative or caregiver helping an older parent with phones, accounts, video calls, home networks, scam prevention and connected devices. The account owner should remain involved and understand every change.

Who is responsible

The Parent Tech Checklist editorial desk selects topics, checks safety wording, reviews primary official sources and maintains the public status rules. Pages identify the latest review date and link to the source material used for that topic.

Corrections and source changes are handled through the published correction process rather than silently changing a safety claim.

What the site does not do

Parent Tech Checklist does not provide medical, legal, financial, cybersecurity or emergency advice. It does not request passwords, recovery codes, private account details, medical records, addresses or emergency contacts. A checklist or consumer device cannot guarantee safety, service availability or scam prevention.

How the work is funded

Free guides and checklists are supported by the site's own printable kit and a limited number of clearly disclosed affiliate links. Commercial relationships do not override the checklist method, safety limits or requirement to verify official sources.
