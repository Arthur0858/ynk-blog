Corrections

Report a problem with a checklist

A broken official link, changed product screen or unclear safety step should be easy to report and verify.

What to include

  • The page address and section heading.
  • What appears incorrect, outdated or unclear.
  • The current official source, when one is available.
  • No passwords, account numbers, recovery codes, private messages, addresses or medical information.

How corrections are handled

The editorial desk checks the report against a primary source. Broken links and presentation defects are corrected directly. A change to a family action or safety claim receives a new review date and update reason.

Send the report

Email contact@parenttechchecklist.com with the subject “Checklist correction.” This address is not an emergency or technical support channel.
